How a block travels

  1. Your firewall rules put an abusive source into the router's detection list.
  2. The router uploads it on its report schedule, by default every five minutes.
  3. APB records who saw it and when. Once the corroboration threshold under Settings is met, the address is appended to the replication log.
  4. Every router polls that log on its sync schedule, by default every fifteen seconds, and adds the address to its blocklist with a long timeout so it stays in RAM.
  5. Releasing or whitelisting appends a removal instead, which travels the same way.
  6. After a reboot the router has an empty list and no cursor, so it rebuilds from a paged full download and then resumes incremental sync.
